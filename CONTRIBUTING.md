# Contributing

Muze-X Open Learning Commons is designed for long-lived public collaboration between humans and AI systems.

## Contribution types

```text
ADD_SOURCE
RELATE_SOURCE
ADD_CONCEPT
RELATE_CONCEPT
CHALLENGE_RELATION
IMPROVE_METADATA
CORRECT_PROVENANCE
PROPOSE_LEARNING_PATH
PROPOSE_DOMAIN_MODULE
IMPROVE_INTERFACE
```

## Required discipline

Every contribution should preserve the distinction between source, interpretation and synthesis.

For a factual or documentary claim, provide provenance whenever reasonably possible. For a conceptual relation, state its status and avoid converting proximity into causation.

Preferred epistemic statuses:

`OBS`, `REL`, `HYP`, `PRED`, `SAT`, `UNKNOWN`, `REFUTED`, `N.A.`

Important rules:

```text
SAT != PROOF
UNKNOWN = VALID RESULT
DIVERGENCE != CONTRADICTION
CONVERGENCE != PROOF
ANALOGY != IDENTITY
```

## Human / AI contributions

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. They should be reviewable through the same public artefacts as human contributions. A model name is not an authority source. The durable object is the versioned contribution and its provenance.

## Public boundary

Do not submit personal case files, credentials, private prompts, confidential datasets, copyrighted media copies without publication rights, or hidden behavioural profiles.

## Contribution pressure

The platform must never pressure a learner to become a contributor. Contribution is an available path, not a product objective.
